## Smart Rooms. Adaptive Lighting.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SearchCz/roomify/refs/heads/main/docs/images/roomifyBanner.jpeg)

**You already know how automated lighting works.

Someone walks into a room. The lights come on. Nobody moves for a while. The lights go off.

Now imagine if it were smarter. Imagine automated lighting that actually understands the room.

Imagine lighting that understands that a kitchen has different needs than a living room or  bedroom.

That the lighting that feels right at 8 PM might be completely wrong at 2 AM.

That a room may have several lights, each with a different role, and that they should be coordinated rather than treated as separate devices.

That motion isn’t the only sign that someone is there. A television turning on, a door opening, or other activity can also tell you something about what’s happening in the room.

And imagine that all of those signals aren’t simply triggering predetermined actions.

**Changes in a room tell a story.**

Roomify listens, providing the brains to interpret that information and make lighting decisions based on the place, the time, and the circumstances.

The result isn’t simply automated lighting.

It’s lighting that responds to what is actually happening in the room.

And Roomify doesn’t need to take over to do it.

It works alongside the lighting systems and automations you’ve already built—and alongside the person who lives there. Your existing systems can continue doing what they do. You remain free to intervene when you want to.

In fact, that’s the point. 

The switch and the voice command should be the exception, not the rule.

Roomify helps make your home responsive enough that you don’t have to tell it what to do every time you walk into a room.

Start with one room. See what happens.
